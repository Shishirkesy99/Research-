## Individual Contribution

I contributed to the development of the Human Research Ethics Approval Application for the research project **"Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare: Benefits, Challenges, Ethical Considerations and Acceptance of AI Technologies."**

My individual contribution included the following sections:

### 1. Research Objectives

I contributed to developing the research objectives, focusing on:

- identifying the benefits of AI applications in healthcare;
- examining ethical, privacy, security and legal concerns;
- investigating trust and acceptance of AI technologies;
- examining perceptions of AI accuracy, reliability and transparency; and
- identifying approaches for responsible implementation of AI in healthcare.

### 2. Research Questions

I contributed to developing the research questions that investigate:

- the perceived benefits of AI in healthcare;
- ethical, privacy, security and legal concerns;
- factors influencing trust and acceptance;
- perceptions of AI transparency, accuracy and reliability; and
- measures that could support responsible AI implementation.

### 3. Research Methodology

I contributed to the development of the proposed research methodology.

The research was designed as a **quantitative cross-sectional study** using an anonymous online questionnaire. I contributed to defining the participant recruitment approach, proposed sample size, data collection methods and data analysis plan.

### 4. Ethical Considerations

I contributed to the ethical considerations section, including:

- voluntary participation;
- informed consent;
- participant withdrawal;
- minimising potential risks and discomfort;
- privacy and confidentiality;
- fair and respectful treatment;
- inclusivity and cultural sensitivity; and
- honesty and integrity in reporting research findings.

### 5. Data Management

I contributed to the data management section covering:

- secure storage of research data;
- password protection;
- restricted access;
- protection of participant information;
- secure handling of electronic data; and
- secure disposal of data after the required retention period.

### 6. Review and Editing

I also reviewed the ethics application to ensure that the proposed research methods were consistent with the research objectives and that participant privacy, informed consent, confidentiality and data security were appropriately addressed.

## Summary of Contribution

My contribution focused primarily on the **research objectives, research questions, methodology, ethical considerations and data management requirements** of the Human Research Ethics Approval Application.

The contribution was prepared as part of the group's overall ethics application and was reviewed for consistency with the proposed AI in Healthcare research project.
