# Survey Design

## Research Project: AI in Healthcare

This document outlines the design of the survey developed to support our group's research project on the adoption, perception, and impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in healthcare settings.

---

## 1. Objectives of the Survey

The primary objective of this survey is to gather first-hand data on how healthcare professionals, patients, and/or the general public perceive, use, and trust AI-driven tools in healthcare (e.g., diagnostic algorithms, AI chatbots, predictive analytics, robotic-assisted procedures, administrative automation).

Specifically, the survey aims to:

1. Measure current levels of awareness and usage of AI tools in healthcare among respondents.
2. Assess the perceived benefits of AI in healthcare (e.g., improved diagnostic accuracy, faster service, reduced costs).
3. Identify perceived risks, concerns, or barriers to AI adoption (e.g., data privacy, loss of human touch, trust in accuracy, job displacement).
4. Understand the factors that influence trust and willingness to rely on AI-supported medical decisions.
5. Gather qualitative insight into personal experiences or expectations regarding AI in healthcare, to complement the quantitative findings.

These objectives are directly aligned with our project's overall research goal of evaluating the real-world adoption, trust, and impact of AI technologies within the healthcare sector, providing empirical data to support (or challenge) findings from our literature review.

---

## 2. How the Survey Will Be Conducted and Distributed

**Method of Administration:**
The survey will be conducted as an **online questionnaire**, created and hosted using Google Forms (or Microsoft Forms). An online format was chosen because it:
- Allows for efficient, low-cost distribution to a geographically dispersed sample.
- Enables automatic collection and partial pre-processing of quantitative data.
- Offers respondents flexibility and anonymity, which may encourage more honest responses on sensitive topics such as trust in AI and data privacy.

**Distribution Strategy:**
The survey link will be distributed through multiple channels to maximize reach and reduce sampling bias:
- **Email:** Sent to university staff, healthcare contacts, and student networks with a brief explanation of the study's purpose.
- **Social media:** Shared via group members' personal and professional networks (e.g., LinkedIn, Facebook, WhatsApp groups) to reach both healthcare professionals and general public respondents.
- **In-class/campus distribution:** Shared with peers and classmates via QR code and direct link during lectures or campus events, to boost response rate among the student demographic.
- **Snowball sampling:** Respondents will be encouraged to forward the survey to relevant contacts (e.g., colleagues in the healthcare sector), which is particularly useful for reaching a specialized population such as healthcare professionals.

The survey will remain open for approximately **2 weeks**, with a reminder sent after the first week to improve response rates.

---

## 3. Selected Sample for the Survey

**Target Population:** Individuals aged 18 and above who either work in the healthcare sector (doctors, nurses, allied health professionals, administrators) or who have interacted with healthcare services as patients or caregivers, along with general members of the public with an interest in or awareness of healthcare technology.

**Expected Sample Size:** A minimum of **100 respondents**, with a target of 150–200, to allow for meaningful quantitative analysis (e.g., cross-tabulations by age, profession, or prior AI exposure) while remaining feasible given the group's time and resource constraints.

**Sampling Method:** A combination of **convenience sampling** (using accessible networks) and **snowball sampling** (respondents referring others), given the group's limited time frame and lack of access to a formal sampling frame.

**Participant Characteristics:**
- Mix of healthcare professionals and general public/patients, to capture both provider- and consumer-side perspectives.
- A range of age groups, to explore whether generational differences affect trust and adoption of AI.
- Varying levels of prior exposure to or familiarity with AI technologies.

## 4. Structure of the Survey

The survey consists of 8 questions: 7 close-ended (quantitative) questions and 1 open-ended (qualitative) question, in addition to a brief introduction and consent statement.

**Introduction (shown before Q1):**
*"This survey is part of a student research project exploring perceptions of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in healthcare. It should take approximately 5 minutes to complete. Your responses are anonymous and will be used solely for academic purposes. By proceeding, you consent to participate in this study."*

**Q1. What is your age group?** *(Close-ended, single choice)*
- Under 18

- 25–34
- 35–44
- 45–54
- 55 and above

**Q2. Which of the following best describes you?** *(Close-ended, single choice)*
- Healthcare professional (e.g., doctor, nurse, allied health worker)
- Patient / general public
- Student (healthcare-related field)
- Student (non-healthcare-related field)
- Other

### Section B: Awareness and Usage

**Q3. How familiar are you with the use of AI technologies in healthcare (e.g., diagnostic tools, AI chatbots, predictive analytics)?** *(Close-ended, 5-point Likert scale)*
- Not at all familiar
- Slightly familiar
- Moderately familiar
- Very familiar
- Extremely familiar

**Q4. Have you personally used or encountered any AI-based healthcare tool or service (e.g., symptom-checker apps, AI-assisted diagnosis, health chatbots)?** *(Close-ended, single choice)*

**Q5. To what extent do you agree that AI improves the accuracy and speed of medical diagnosis?** *(Close-ended, 5-point Likert scale: Strongly Disagree – Strongly Agree)*

## 5. Ethical Considerations

Several ethical considerations will be taken into account when conducting and distributing the survey. Participation will be voluntary, and participants will be informed about the purpose of the research before completing the survey. Participants will not be pressured to participate and will have the option to stop completing the survey at any time.

The survey will avoid collecting unnecessary personal or sensitive information. Responses will be kept anonymous where possible, and participants will not be required to provide identifying information such as their name or contact details. The information collected will only be used for the purposes of this research project and will not be shared with unauthorized individuals.

The research group will also ensure that participants' responses are stored securely and accessed only by authorized group members. Survey questions will be written respectfully and will avoid offensive, discriminatory, or unnecessarily sensitive content. These measures will help protect participants' privacy, maintain confidentiality, and ensure that the research is conducted responsibly.

Several ethical considerations were addressed in the design and administration of this survey:

1. **Informed Consent:** Participants are informed of the survey's purpose, estimated duration, and voluntary nature before beginning. Proceeding with the survey constitutes informed consent.

2. **Anonymity and Confidentiality:** No personally identifiable information (e.g., name, email address, contact details) is collected. Responses are stored securely and reported only in aggregate form, ensuring individual respondents cannot be identified in the final research output.

3. **Voluntary Participation and Right to Withdraw:** Participation is entirely voluntary. Respondents may skip any question (except mandatory screening questions) or exit the survey at any point without penalty.

4. **No Harm to Participants:** The survey topic (perceptions of AI in healthcare) is low-risk and non-sensitive in nature. No questions probe personal medical history, diagnoses, or other sensitive health information.

5. **Data Storage and Use:** Collected data will be stored securely (e.g., password-protected Google account) and used solely for the purposes of this academic research project. Data will not be shared with third parties or used for commercial purposes, and will be deleted or anonymized after project completion in line with our institution's research data guidelines.

6. **Avoiding Bias:** Care has been taken to phrase questions neutrally (avoiding leading language) so as not to unduly influence respondents' answers, particularly on questions involving trust and risk perception.

7. **Transparency:** Contact information for the research group (or supervising instructor, if applicable) will be made available at the end of the survey should participants have questions or wish to withdraw their data after submission.
